SMART PARK — Digital Slot Management

An object-oriented digital parking platform featuring real-time slot availability, QR-based contactless verification, transactional double-booking prevention, and automated billing.

Accurate timestamp duration tracking, configurable minimum billing hours, and simulated instant electronic payment processing.
